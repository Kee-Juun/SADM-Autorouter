# Out of Spec: Visual Canon

This file records visual facts that generated or hand-authored game assets must preserve.
Treat these details as continuity requirements, not optional prompt flavor.

## Named Cast

- **Elian Vey:** Southeast Asian man, early 30s, medium-brown skin, practical black hair,
  tired asymmetrical face, crooked nose. Carries a brass hourglass key with a chipped blue stone.
- **Lysa Marr:** Black woman, late 30s, deep-brown skin, broad practical face, loosely pinned
  natural coiled hair, ink-stained hands. Associated with a quill, copied docket, and east desk.
- **Orren Vale:** Middle Eastern man, late 50s, olive-brown skin, receding salt-and-pepper hair,
  weathered asymmetrical face. Wears restrained black-and-gold chancellor robes and a Black Sun seal.
- **Tomas Rook:** White Eastern European man, mid-40s, stocky build, fair ruddy skin,
  thinning brown hair, healed crooked nose, soot-marked work clothes, and an iron bell key.

Characters are ordinary working people. Avoid glamour casting, airbrushed skin, romantic-cover
posing, ethnic stereotypes, and unexplained changes to age, build, facial structure, clothing, or props.

## Level One Character Focus

Level One introduces the named cast. Only the Last Clerk, Mara, Elian Vey, Lysa Marr,
Orren Vale, and Tomas Rook may receive readable faces or visually dominant character staging.
Do not invent identifiable side characters merely to populate a scene. When human scale is
strictly necessary in the background, use distant hooded or shrouded silhouettes with fully
obscured faces and no distinctive props, expressions, or story emphasis. Prefer an empty room,
an autonomous mechanism, or an anonymous gloved hand when those choices communicate the same beat.

## Magical Color Language

- **Bellglass Fire:** unnatural blue only. Flames, reflected light, smoke edges, and fire damage
  reconstructions connected to the Bellglass event must retain blue illumination.
- **Keeper's Hourglass:** crystalline blue; suspends time without hiding the record.
- **Registry Crown:** green-gold; transfers a record between visible public offices.
- **Ashen Bell:** amber-gold barrier activated after the blue Bellglass Fire; it protects damaged records.
- **Chancellor's Black Sun:** black-violet; lowers an active record below public review.

## Lower Registry Continuity

The recovered chronology is fixed:

1. Vey discovers names disappearing from ink while wax impressions remain.
2. Marr creates and hides one surviving copy at the east desk.
3. Vale authorizes the Black Sun transfer.
4. Blue Bellglass Fire reaches the western stacks and Rook sounds the warning bell.

Generated animation rows must preserve camera geography, character identity, costume, props,
and object placement between adjacent frames. Movement may advance; the world must not redesign itself.
